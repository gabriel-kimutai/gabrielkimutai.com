## Bio

I am a software developer / engineer. I work on backend systems, mobile applications, and the infrastructure that keeps services deployable and operable.

Day to day that means Go and API work, React/TypeScript where a web client is needed, Android/Kotlin for mobile systems, and Linux/cloud tooling around delivery. I am particularly interested in networking and distributed systems — service discovery, realtime channels, and clear service boundaries.

I care about building software that solves real problems with a shape you can explain: explicit interfaces, reliable APIs, and systems you can observe when something breaks.
